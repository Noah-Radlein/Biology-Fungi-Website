Parts of a Fungus

Fungi are unique organisms that play an important role in nature. They obtain nutrients by absorbing them from their surroundings and reproduce through spores.

Cap

The top structure of a mushroom that protects reproductive tissues.

Gills

Thin structures found under the cap that produce spores.

Stem (Stipe)

The stalk that supports the cap.

Ring

A thin membrane that surrounds the stem.

Mycelium

A network of fungal threads found underground.

Volva

A cup-like structure at the base of some mushrooms.
